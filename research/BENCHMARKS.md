## Principles taken (not copied)

1. Lead with an idea and a real photograph, not a ranking banner. Rankings appear as sourced facts.
2. The first decision on the homepage is a working programme finder (audience → programme), not a link list.
3. One brand colour may own entire regions. Here burgundy owns editorial fields; IIMB red marks the institution (logo, primary actions, "you are here").
4. Chrome stays to one row; the full site map lives in a designed contents overlay.
5. Faculty directory: free-text search, area filters, A–Z index, portraits.
6. Every video has a poster and a phone fallback.
7. Research needs at least one visual built from real data (here: working papers by year and theme).
