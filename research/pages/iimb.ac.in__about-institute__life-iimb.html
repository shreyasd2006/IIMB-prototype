Life @IIMB

Campus & Architecture

Placed in the heart of India’s knowledge capital, IIM Bangalore houses iconic structures that make our campus an architectural embodiment of both legacy and innovation.

Architecture

The 54,000 sq mt IIMB complex, built on a 100-acre campus, is based on the design of the town of Fatehpur Sikri

Dr. B V Doshi

The IIMB campus was designed by celebrated architect B V Doshi. The campus is a destination and a pilgrimage for students of architecture and practising architects, with the architecture of the academic and administrative blocks becoming a case study. Completed in 1983, the original stone architecture is now complemented by the greenery, just as B V Doshi had intended.

Learn More About Architecture


Campus Tour

Campus tours immerse you in the fabled history of the iconic stone walls. Have a look into the enduring ecosystem of living and learning that has established IIMB as a beacon of education for over five decades.


Auditorium & Library

IIM Bangalore boasts one of the finest auditoriums for teaching of all kinds, offering a top-notch learning environment. The library is home to an exceptional collection of world-class books by renowned authors, a rarity in most institutions


Sustainability

Sustainability lies at the heart of our infrastructure. With rooftop solar installations, innovative water management systems, and zero-waste initiatives, IIMB is geared towards making ‘greener living’ an integral part of its future endeavors.

Student Life, Activities and Organizations 

Life at IIMB extends far beyond the classroom.

With over 1500 student-run clubs and organizations, there is something for everyone—whether you are passionate about finance, entrepreneurship, sports, the arts, or everything in between. Our students are encouraged to explore their interests, build networks, develop leadership skills and in the meantime form lifelong friendships through an abundance of extracurricular activities.
