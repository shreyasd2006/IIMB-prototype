## About IIMB Management Review
IMR, IIMB’s flagship quarterly journal, is consistently a peer leader in India and a leading management journal in the Asian region. Currently on its 38 th Volume, IMR is now a gold open access Elsevier journal registering strong metrics across various parameters.

####
Aims and Scope
Addressed to management practitioners, researchers and academics, IMR aims to engage rigorously with practices, concepts and ideas in the ﬁeld of management, with an emphasis on providing managerial insights, in a format that is accessible to a wide audience. To this end, IMR invites manuscripts that provide novel managerial insights in any of the core business functions.

Publication Information
IIMB Management Review (ISSN 0970 3896) is published quarterly. All editorial correspondence should be addressed to the Editor at the Indian Institute of Management, Bannerghatta Road, Bengaluru 560 076 or sent by email to eo[at]iimb[dot]ac[dot]in . All papers must be submitted via the journal’s online submission website ( http://ees.elsevier.com/iimb/ ). (refer to Guidelines for Contributors for details)
