# IIM Bangalore announces PhD Fellowship recipients for 2026-27 and Doctoral Student Research Paper Award recipients for 2026
{IMG /sites/default/files/2026-10/Campus%201.jpg}
14 doctoral students received scholarship and fellowship awards
12 October, 2026, Bangalore: The Office of the [https://www.iimb.ac.in/programmes/doctoral-programme] Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) Programme at IIM Bangalore has announced the recipients of the PhD Fellowships for 2026-27 and the Doctoral Student Research Paper Awards for 2026.
Congratulating the awardees, [https://www.iimb.ac.in/user/191/tarun-jain] Prof. Tarun Jain , Chairperson, Doctor of Philosophy (PhD), said, “On behalf of the Doctoral Programme, I wish them all the very best and look forward to great research and publications in top-tier journals from them.”
PhD Student Scholarship winners
The PhD Fellowship Awards are presented based on the students’ academic performance, research productivity, publications in reputed journals, and quality of ongoing research.
The PhD Fellowship recipients for 2026-27 are: Ronit Neogy and Mantu Prasad Gupta of the Decision Sciences area (Mirae Asset Scholars Award); Kalaga Venkata Laxmana Tejaswi of the Marketing area and Gunjesh Kumar of the Finance & Accounting area (WIPRO Sustainability Fellowship); Mauli Kundlia of the OBHRM area (JR Fellowship Award); and Shweta Sogani and Pradyun Rame Mehrotra of the Economics area and Saheli Bose of the Production & Operations Management area (Florintree Scholarship Award).
Doctoral Student Research Paper Award 2026
The Doctoral Student Research Paper Awards recognise research papers accepted in reputed journals during the academic year 2025-26.
This year’s recipients are P. M. Naveen, OBHRM area; Sanchit Jain, Finance & Accounting area; Divya Dwivedi, Information Systems area; Shweta Sogani, Economics area; Anubha Shokhand, Strategy area and Sukanya Roy, Entrepreneurship area.
