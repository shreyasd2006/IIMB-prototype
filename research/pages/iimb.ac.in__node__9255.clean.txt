# Student-run initiative Prayaas invites school students to pitch ideas for 'Youth Udyami 2027'
{IMG /sites/default/files/2026-10/IIMB-Main-Gate_1_0_0.jpg}
EPGP, supported by PGPEM, opens Stage 1 submissions for its youth entrepreneurship and mentorship program for Classes 9 to 12
06 October, 2026, Bangalore: Prayaas, the social responsibility initiative of students of the [https://www.iimb.ac.in/programmes/epgp] Executive Post Graduate Programme in Management (EPGP) , IIM Bangalore's one-year, full-time MBA for experienced professionals, and supported by the [https://www.iimb.ac.in/programmes/pgpem] Post Graduate Programme in Enterprise Management (PGPEM) , has announced 'Youth Udyami 2027', a youth entrepreneurship program at IIMB. The initiative aims to foster entrepreneurship among school students from Classes 9 to 12, empowering them to become self-reliant catalysts for India's economic development.
To register now, please click here: [https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfZUN4gZMyiUQfuQS_3ENaUrBfzU7oldYtCrHHaPmcIoEWbHQ/viewform] https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfZUN4gZMyiUQfuQS_3ENaUrBfzU7oldYtCrHHaPmcIoEWbHQ/viewform
Last date for registration and Stage 1 submission is on 01 November 2026.
Selected teams will be mentored by EPGP and PGPEM students, who bring several years of industry experience across sectors. The mentorship will offer insights into industry best practices, enabling participants to apply these learnings during the project and in their future ventures. The program will culminate at Samvay 2027, EPGP's business conclave, tentatively scheduled for January 2027, where final presentations will be evaluated by a panel of IIMB faculty and eminent industry leaders.
Theme: 'From idea to impact to income'
Youth Udyami 2027 will engage students in a structured program of business planning, hands-on activities and market validation, through which participants will conceptualise and develop a functional startup model over the coming months. Students are encouraged to identify areas they are genuinely passionate about, whether sports, travel, technology, arts, fashion, food or any other interest, and develop them into a profitable business idea. The goal is to align personal interests with entrepreneurial thinking, innovation and market viability.
The program also welcomes ideas in social entrepreneurship, encouraging participants to build models that address societal issues while demonstrating self-sustainability and impact-driven profitability.
Participants are required to identify a problem they have personally experienced or observed, or one faced by friends or family members, and propose a practical solution and a corresponding business idea. The concept may be product-based or service-oriented.
Participation criteria
Youth Udyami is open to students currently enrolled in Classes 9, 10, 11 and 12, across all academic streams, in recognised government and private schools across India. Teams may comprise up to three members. Each school may nominate a maximum of three teams, and all teams will be evaluated independently.
A registration fee of ₹500 per team is payable at the time of registration. It covers participation in all phases of the program, access to mentorship, and certificates for all team members.
Participants are expected to make their own travel and accommodation arrangements; limited financial aid may be extended to Grand Finale teams on a need-cum-merit basis, with details shared with shortlisted teams.
Program stages
Stage 1 : Idea Pitch Round
Teams submit a video of up to two minutes (max) describing their motivation, vision and idea. Submissions open on 7 October 2026 and close on 1 November 2026. Shortlisting will be based on the clarity and relevance of the problem and the feasibility and impact of the proposed solution. Up to 50 teams will advance to Stage 2.
Stage 2 : Idea Defence Round (Virtual)
In this virtual round, students will pitch their idea, its impact and its feasibility. Teams will deliver a seven-minute pitch followed by a three-minute Q&A with the judging panel. Each team will also submit at least five customer interviews validating the relevance and depth of the problem, and a completed Lean Canvas in the format provided by the organising team. The top 10 teams will proceed to the Grand Finale.
Grand Finale : On-Campus Final Pitch
From 7 December 2026, finalist teams will be mentored by EPGP and PGPEM students to refine their ideas. In January 2027, teams will present polished, investor-ready business ideas on the IIMB campus, alongside Samvay 2027, to a panel of IIMB professors and industry leaders. Each finalist team may be accompanied by one guardian, who may be a teacher or a parent of one of the selected participant.
All Grand Finale teams will receive a Certificate of Merit. One team will be declared the winner, while the remaining finalists will receive consolation prizes. Details of the awards will be announced closer to the Grand Finale.
Overview and Timeline (Tentative)
7 October 2026 : Registrations open. Stage 1 submissions begin.
1 November 2026 : Last date for registration and Stage 1 submissions.
6 November 2026 : Stage 1 results announced; up to 50 teams shortlisted for Stage 2.
9 November 2026 : Stage 2 (Idea Defence Round, virtual) begins.
3 December 2026 : Top 10 teams announced.
7 December 2026 : Mentoring begins for the finalist teams.
16 January 2027: Grand Finale at IIM Bangalore.
For queries, write to the Prayaas team at [mailto:prayaas@iimb.ac.in] prayaas@iimb.ac.in .
