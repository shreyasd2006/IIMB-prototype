# PGP, PGPBA and EPGP sweep No. 1 positions in India in QS MiM, BA and Global MBA Rankings 2027
{IMG /sites/default/files/2026-09/IMG_8274.jpg}
PGP ranked #31 globally in Master’s in Management; PGPBA and EPGP secured global top-100 positions
17 September, 2026, Bengaluru : IIM Bangalore’s [https://www.iimb.ac.in/programmes/pgp] Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) , [https://www.iimb.ac.in/programmes/pgpba] Post Graduate Programme in Business Analytics (PGPBA) and the [https://www.iimb.ac.in/programmes/epgp] Executive Post Graduate Programme in Management (EPGP) have once again secured the No. 1 positions in India in their respective categories in the QS Business Master's and Global MBA Rankings for 2027, featuring prominently among leading management education programmes worldwide.
Globally, the PGP is ranked #31 in the QS Master’s in Management (MiM) 2027 rankings; the PGPBA secured #60 in the QS Master’s in Business Analytics rankings; and EPGP ranked #64 globally and #8 in the Asia-Pacific region in the QS Global MBA 2027 rankings.
PGP: #31 globally in QS Master’s in Management
IIMB’s two-year fulltime MBA, PGP, secured the #31 position globally and #1 in India in the QS Master’s in Management 2027 rankings, with an overall score of 72.7.
The PGP recorded particularly strong scores across key indicators of academic standing, employability and value for money. It achieved 83.6 in Employer Reputation & Employment Rate and secured the highest Employability Rank in the country at #18. In Thought Leadership, which evaluates Academic Reputation, Research Impact and PhD Faculty, the PGP scored 83.1 and ranked #20 globally, again the highest in the country. Its overall Value for Money score stood at 66.0, with Return on Investment carrying 15% weightage in the ranking methodology. Under Alumni Outcomes , which evaluates Alumni Impact , the PGP scored 70.3.
“PGP’s performance in the QS rankings, following its recognition as the No. 1 MBA in the country in the FT MiM 2026 rankings, speaks to the strength of an education that brings together applied business learning, evidence-led teaching, industry engagement, and the development of leaders equipped to navigate complex business and societal challenges”, said Prof. [https://www.iimb.ac.in/user/70/u-dinesh-kumar] U Dinesh Kumar , Director-in-charge, IIMB.
PGPBA: #60 globally in QS Master’s in Business Analytics
PGPBA secured #60 globally in the QS Master’s in Business Analytics 2027 rankings with a score of 60.3.
Under Employer Reputation , the programme recorded a score of 84.8 and a global rank of #16, placing its performance strongly on one of the key dimensions of the ranking. Under the Thought Leadership parameter, the programme secured a score of 72.6.
The two-year fulltime programme brings together management education and advanced analytics, preparing graduates to work at the intersection of business, data, technology, and decision-making.
EPGP: #64 globally in QS Global MBA rankings
EPGP ranked #64 globally in the QS Global MBA 2027 rankings and #8 in the Asia-Pacific region.
The one-year fulltime MBA designed for experienced professionals recorded a strong 78.3 score in Employability , the highest in the country, which carries 40% of the weight, while its Thought Leadership score stood at 71.5.
Meanwhile, in the QS EMBA Rankings 2026, released earlier this year, IIMB’s two-year MBA for Working Professionals – the [https://www.iimb.ac.in/programmes/pgpem] Post Graduate Programme in Enterprise Management (PGPEM) – remained the No. 1 Executive MBA in India and was the only Indian business school to feature among the global top 100.
The Diversity parameter emerged as a common area of progress, with all four MBA programmes improving their gender ratio and international representation among faculty and students.
The QS Business Master's and Global MBA rankings evaluate programmes across dimensions including employability, alumni outcomes, thought leadership, diversity, and measures of return on investment/value for money, depending on the ranking category. The Institute’s continued representation across global ranking formats reinforces its commitment to creating knowledge, nurturing leaders and advancing societal value through excellence in management education and research.
