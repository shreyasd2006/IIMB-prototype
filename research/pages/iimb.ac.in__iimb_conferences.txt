Research & Publications Office to host seminar on ‘Does Bank Lending to Nonbanks Affect Credit Allocation and Systemic Risk?’
Talk by Prof. Abhishek Bhardwaj, Indian School of Business, Hyderabad
13 October 2026, Bengaluru: The Office of Research and Publications at IIM Bangalore will host a research seminar titled ‘Does Bank Lending to Nonbanks Affect Credit Allocation and Systemic Risk?’, featuring Prof. Abhishek Bhardwaj, Assistant Professor of Finance at the Indian School of Business (ISB), Hyderabad. The seminar will be held on 22 October 2026, at 12:00 pm, in Classroom Q-001 at IIMB.
Abstract
The study examines the causal effects of bank lending to non-bank financial institutions (NBFIs) using administrative loan-level data. Exploiting a regulatory reform that eased capital requirements for bank exposures to NBFIs, the study finds that increased bank funding enables NBFIs to expand credit to smaller, younger, and riskier firms, thereby promoting financial inclusion and allocative efficiency.
However, evidence from the failure of a large NBFI reveals that bank–NBFI linkages can also amplify systemic risk. Banks exposed to the failed institution reduce lending to unrelated firms and NBFIs, which, in turn, curtail lending to their own borrowers, propagating credit contractions through the financial system.
The findings highlight the dual role of bank–NBFI linkages: while they expand credit access and improve credit allocation, they can also transmit financial distress through interconnected lending relationships.
Speaker Profile
Prof. Abhishek Bhardwaj is an Assistant Professor of Finance at the Indian School of Business. He holds a PhD in Finance from the NYU Stern School of Business.
His research examines how financial institutions shape credit allocation, financial stability, and real economic outcomes, with a focus on banking, private equity, and the growing role of non-bank financial institutions in modern financial systems. Another stream of his research explores how firms adapt to climate change and natural disasters, particularly in relation to employment, investment, and productivity.
His research has been published or accepted in leading finance and economics journals, including the Review of Financial Studies, Journal of Political Economy: Macroeconomics, and Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis. His work has received research grants from organisations such as the National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER) and has been presented at leading academic, policy, and industry forums. He teaches corporate finance at the graduate level.
Prof. Bhardwaj also serves as an Independent Director at Zerodha Trustee Private Limited, focusing on governance, risk oversight, and regulatory issues associated with the growth of passive investing and retail participation in financial markets. Prior to entering academia, he worked at Deutsche Bank, where he engaged with financial markets and hedge fund clients.
His research combines large-scale data with modern empirical methods to address questions relevant to regulators, financial institutions, investors, and policymakers, bridging academic research, financial practice, and public policy.

Centre for Teaching & Learning to host seventh cohort of Nurturing Future Leadership Programme (NFLP)
The five-day residential programme for HEI faculty members will be held from 2-6 November 2026
22 September, 2026, Bengaluru: The Centre for Teaching and Learning (CTL) at IIMB will host the seventh cohort of the Nurturing Future Leadership Programme (NFLP) from 2nd to 6th November 2026. Supported by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, the five-day residential programme is designed for faculty members in higher educational institutions who are aspiring to take on academic leadership roles.
The programme is being offered in the context of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, which emphasizes capacity building among faculty members of higher educational institutions and the development of future academic leaders.
Eligible participants include full-time faculty members of centrally funded institutes, state public universities/institutes and private higher educational institutions, with the number of seats determined as per MMTTP norms. Faculty members from recognized institutions must be nominated by their Director, Vice Chancellor or Head of Institution and submit the nomination letter through the Malaviya Mission Teacher Training Programme (MMTTC) portal.
The NFLP aims to help faculty members in the early stages of their careers develop the capabilities needed to emerge as effective academic leaders. The programme will focus on areas including leadership and institution building, leadership and teamwork, strategic thinking and planning, decision-making, building centres of excellence in academia, fostering equity and inclusivity, institutional legal aspects, entrepreneurial ecosystems in higher education institutions, digital learning capacity and communicating for impact.
The sessions will be led by senior and eminent faculty members of IIM Bangalore and will combine lectures, video-based lectures, in-class activities and case discussions. Five sessions will be conducted each day, with each session lasting 75 minutes. Participants who successfully complete the programme will receive a certificate of participation, with attendance across all five days being mandatory.
The programme will be held at IIM Bangalore’s Bannerghatta Road campus. For more details, view the programme brochure.
For further information, reach out to the Centre for Teaching and Learning at ctl@iimb.ac.in or call 080-26993852.

GIFT Finance Olympiad: National Student Case Challenge 2026

GIFT Finance Olympiad: National Student Case Challenge 2026
Organized by the Centre for Capital Markets and Risk Management (CCMRM) at IIM Bangalore, in collaboration with GIFT City
14 October, 2026, Bengaluru: The Centre for Capital Markets and Risk Management (CCMRM) at IIM Bangalore, in collaboration with GIFT City, invites postgraduate students across India to participate in the GIFT Finance Olympiad: National Student Case Challenge 2026. The competition offers students an opportunity to engage with a real-world case drawn from GIFT City’s financial infrastructure and develop practical, evidence-based solutions to questions shaping India’s international financial markets.
Registrations are open from 14th October 2026. Interested students are requested to review the instructions below and complete the registration using the following link: CCMRM, IIM Bangalore × GIFT City Finance Competition 2026 – Fill in form
The competition will unfold in three stages: a national online case challenge, in-person regional finals in Bengaluru, Delhi, Kolkata and Mumbai, and a Grand Finale at GIFT City, Gandhinagar. Through this progression, participants will have the opportunity to apply their academic learning, examine real market and institutional trade-offs, and present and defend their recommendations before a panel of academic and industry experts.
About the Competition
GIFT City’s International Financial Services Centre (IFSC) is emerging as a gateway for offshore banking, capital markets, aircraft leasing, fund management and sustainable finance. As its institutions evolve and engage with global financial centres, questions of market design, product innovation, governance and institutional strategy become increasingly important.
The GIFT Finance Olympiad invites students to examine these questions through an applied case centred on GIFT City’s financial ecosystem. Participants are expected to go beyond conceptual solutions and assess the commercial rationale, technical feasibility, implementation challenges and risks associated with their recommendations. The emphasis is on sound analysis, independent thinking and solutions that strengthen GIFT IFSC’s long-term credibility and competitiveness.
Eligibility
The competition is open to postgraduate students enrolled in any recognized college, university or B-school in India, irrespective of discipline.
Each team must comprise four students, with interdisciplinary teams particularly encouraged. Students from finance, economics, technology, data and related fields are welcome to collaborate. Each student may participate in only one team.
Why participate?
Engage with a live, real-world question facing India's offshore financial centre.
Present before exchange officials, bankers and academics.
Interact with practitioners from GIFT IFSC institutions and IIM Bangalore faculty.
Compete for cash prizes and national recognition.
City winners will advance to the Grand Finale at GIFT City, Gandhinagar.
Competition Format
The competition will be conducted in three stages.
Stage I: National Case Challenge (Online)
Teams will solve a real-world GIFT City financial infrastructure case.
Teams will choose one of two analytical tracks.
Each team will submit a deck comprising one title slide and up to four content slides.
Submissions will be evaluated by a panel comprising academics and industry practitioners.
Stage II: Regional Finals (In-Person)
Regional Finals will be held in four cities. Shortlisted teams will compete in a single-day, in-person round comprising a case extension and a live presentation with judge Q&A. The winning team from each regional city will advance to the Grand Finale at GIFT City.
21st November 2026 – Delhi
28th November 2026 – Bengaluru
30th November 2026 – Kolkata
5th December 2026 – Mumbai
Stage III: Grand Finale – GIFT City, Gandhinagar
The four regional winners will be invited to GIFT City, Gandhinagar for the Grand Finale. Finalist teams will receive a fresh, more detailed case extension on the same theme, to be solved under time constraints and presented live to the judging panel, including a Q&A.
The date of the Grand Finale will be announced separately.
Analytical Tracks
Track 1 – Markets & Product Design
GIFT Nifty has successfully consolidated offshore trading interest in Nifty index derivatives onto an IFSC platform. Building on this, suppose NSE IX is evaluating the launch of single-stock futures on a basket of large Indian companies (via depository receipt-linked structures) for trading by global investors out of GIFT IFSC.
Your proposal should clearly identify and justify:
Target participants and eligibility (FPIs, NRIs, global institutions).
Contract design (underlying, lot size, settlement type – cash vs physical).
Clearing, margining and counterparty risk framework.
Liquidity-provision mechanism (market makers, incentives).
Interoperability with onshore Indian markets and existing GIFT Nifty infrastructure.
Operational challenges and expected benefits to GIFT IFSC's positioning.
Track 2 – Institutional Strategy & Governance
Global exchanges, custodians and clearing corporations are evaluating deeper partnerships, cross-listing arrangements or equity stakes in GIFT IFSC market infrastructure institutions. Develop a governance and strategy framework for how a GIFT IFSC exchange or clearing corporation should evaluate and structure such partnerships.
Your framework should consider:
Strategic rationale (liquidity access, technology, credibility) versus risks of dependence.
Governance structure and board representation for foreign partners.
Data-sharing and cybersecurity considerations.
Competitive positioning against Singapore Exchange, Nasdaq Dubai and Hong Kong Exchanges.
Long-term sustainability of the partnership beyond an initial liquidity boost.
Exit/renegotiation provisions and accountability mechanisms.
What will the jury look for?
Teams should evaluate not only whether their proposed solution is commercially and technically feasible, but also whether it strengthens GIFT IFSC's long-term credibility as a financial centre.
Does the proposed expansion address a genuine market need, or merely chase volume/headlines?
Is the proposed approach necessary, or could the same objective be achieved with a simpler design?
Do the anticipated benefits justify the additional risks taken on?
Under what conditions would the proposal strengthen investor trust, market integrity and GIFT IFSC's global standing over the next decade?
Teams should clearly state all assumptions underlying their recommendations and justify the rationale for their proposed design choices.
Evaluation criterion
Weight
Quality and originality of analysis
30%
Depth of market or institutional strategy thinking (per chosen track)
25%
Feasibility and implementability of recommendations
20%
Understanding of market and strategic trade-offs
15%
Clarity and quality of presentation
10%
AI Use Disclosure
Teams may use AI tools to support research and drafting. However, submissions must include a brief disclosure slide or footnote identifying the tools used and explaining how they were used. Submissions that rely on AI-generated analysis without genuine engagement with GIFT City-specific facts, figures and trade-offs will score poorly against the evaluation criteria, particularly on originality and feasibility. Selection for the regional stage will further test teams' ability to defend and extend their own analysis live, in person.

CSITM invites paper submissions for 'The India Lens: Building Theory from Indian Phenomena', a Scholarly Advancement Mentorship Workshop
Two-day workshop with the AIS India Chapter, led by Prof. Abhay Mishra, will be held on 1 and 2 December 2026
8 October, 2026, October: The Centre for Software and IT Management (CSITM) at IIM Bangalore, in collaboration with the Association for Information Systems (AIS) India Chapter, has announced a two-day Scholarly Advancement Mentorship Workshop titled 'The India Lens: Building Theory from Indian Phenomena'. The workshop will be held on 1 and 2 December 2026 at the Institute campus. The last date for paper submission is 30 October 2026.
Fill this form, to apply now: The India Lens: Building Theory from Indian Phenomena
The workshop is an initiative to nurture scholars, strengthen research capabilities and advance high-quality research towards publication. Through a series of scholarly initiatives and events, CSITM seeks to foster a vibrant research ecosystem in India by offering scholars focused training, mentoring and opportunities for meaningful scholarly engagement.
Call for papers
CSITM and the AIS India Chapter invite submissions that address a research problem, idea or phenomenon rooted in India and employ a rigorous research methodology, such as a multimethod design. Authors must submit a full paper of 7,000 to 8,000 words, as the workshop is designed to advance promising research towards publication.
A selection committee will review all submissions and shortlist five papers for the closed-group workshop on the IIM Bangalore campus. Prof. Abhay Mishra will work closely with the selected authors, providing intensive, hands-on guidance to strengthen their manuscripts and take them towards publication in leading journals.
About Prof. Abhay Mishra

Workshop Overview
The Professional Development Workshop (PDW) is designed to give scholars developmental feedback on sustainability-related International Business (IB) research. It offers a supportive environment in which participants receive constructive comments from experienced researchers, helping them strengthen their manuscripts for submission to leading IB journals such as the Journal of International Business Studies, Journal of World Business, Global Strategy Journal and Journal of International Management.
Sustainability has become a central theme in IB research, spanning climate change, ESG, sustainable global value chains, responsible internationalization, the circular economy, social inclusion, energy transition, biodiversity and the Sustainable Development Goals. South Asia is an important setting for such work because of its rapidly growing economies, institutional diversity, environmental vulnerabilities, expanding multinational activity and increasing integration into global value chains.
Many scholars, particularly doctoral students and early-career researchers, have limited opportunities to receive detailed feedback before submitting to journals. The workshop addresses this gap through a structured mentoring environment in which participants receive in-depth comments from senior scholars with expertise in sustainability and International Business.

Submissions will be evaluated on their relevance to sustainability and International Business, originality, potential academic contribution, and potential for constructive development through the workshop. Preference will be given to papers that would benefit most from developmental feedback.
