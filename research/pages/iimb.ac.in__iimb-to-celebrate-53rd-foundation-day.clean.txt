# IIM Bangalore to celebrate its 53rd Foundation Day with special lecture on ‘India’s Energy Journey: IndianOil’s Role in Securing Energy, Navigating Geopolitics and Building Resilience’
Talk to be delivered by IndianOil Chairman Shri A S Sahney
13 October, 2026, Bengaluru: The 53rd Foundation Day of Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB) will be celebrated on 28th October 2026, with speeches, awards, and more. The IIMB community of students, faculty, former faculty, alumni, staff and officers will be part of the celebrations.
The highlight of the event will be the Foundation Day Lecture on, ‘India’s Energy Journey: IndianOil’s Role in Securing Energy, Navigating Geopolitics and Building Resilience’ by Shri A S Sahney, Chairman, IndianOil.
About the speaker: Chairman of India’s flagship energy major IndianOil, Shri A S Sahney is a Chemical Engineer from HBTU, Kanpur, and brings over three decades of experience in refining and petrochemicals. Championing people-centric leadership, innovation, digital transformation and sustainability as drivers of organizational growth, he is steering IndianOil’s transformation through Project SPRINT, with a strong thrust on biofuels, strengthening India’s energy security while navigating an evolving global energy landscape.
Having worked across five of IndianOil’s nine refineries, he has contributed significantly to major projects, including the landmark 15 MMTPA Paradip Refinery. He also spearheaded the conceptualization of major petrochemical projects, including the upcoming mega Petrochemical Complex at Paradip.
Beyond IndianOil, Shri Sahney serves as Chairman of Chennai Petroleum Corporation Limited (CPCL), the Federation of Indian Petroleum Industry (FIPI) and the CII Public Sector Enterprises (PSE) Council for 2026-27, as well as Vice President of the Paris-based World Liquid Gas Association (WLGA), besides holding several other leadership and Board positions across the energy industry.
A firm believer in people-centric leadership, Shri Sahney champions operational excellence, cost consciousness, customer-centricity, innovation and talent development. Under his leadership, IndianOil is pursuing Project SPRINT, a company-wide transformation initiative to build a more agile, competitive and future-ready organization. Moreover, under his leadership, IndianOil is also accelerating its energy transition journey, with a strong thrust on biofuels, Compressed BioGas (CBG), Green Hydrogen and Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF).
At a time of geopolitical shifts, technological disruption and energy transition, his perspectives on leadership and India’s energy future are particularly relevant to the academic community and aspiring business leaders.
