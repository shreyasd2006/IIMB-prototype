# ‘When every Indian works for national good, we will be truly a super power’: Prof. U Dinesh Kumar
{IMG /sites/default/files/2026-08/DSC02011%281%29%20copy.jpg}
IIMB marks India’s 80th Independence Day with a celebration of patriotism, service and community
15 August, 2026, Bengaluru: The youth on the IIM Bangalore campus made the 80th Independence Day celebrations special with a well-scripted ‘nukkad naatak’ (play) on ‘Nasha Mukt Bharat’, a scintillating fusion dance and a soulful medley of patriotic songs, while two young campus community members gave the audience glimpses of their spectacular musical talent. The morning was all the more special with the presence of alumni of PGP 1991, who were here to mark their 35th reunion.
IIM Bangalore has plenty to be grateful for and celebrate, and what better occasion than the country’s 80th Independence Day! The celebrations opened with an energetic march past by the campus security personnel who were welcomed with loud cheers. The tricolour was then unfurled by Professor [https://www.iimb.ac.in/user/70/u-dinesh-kumar] U Dinesh Kumar , Director-in-charge, IIM Bangalore.
In his special address, Professor Dinesh Kumar paid rich tributes to the country’s freedom fighters and encouraged every member of the IIMB community to contribute to national good. “India is revered for her wisdom, love, compassion and kindness. In times of darkness and despair, India is the shining light,” he said.
His address was followed by awards to recognise outstanding contributions from members of the community. Teams from the Institute’s Estates Office, Hostels, Canteen, and Management Development Centre were recognised and thanked for their tireless work in keeping the IIMB community safe and happy. The IIMB Horticulture team was applauded for winning the second prize for ‘best ornamental campus garden’ in the Independence Day Flower Show, organized by the Horticulture Department of the Government of Karnataka.
The celebrations continued with cultural programmes by members of ‘Dhwani’ and ‘Taal’, the student clubs for music and dance, and culminated with two rousing songs – ‘ee sundara naadu nanna Bharata’ by young Anaya Kudachi and ‘Aye mere pyaare watan’ by Ms Abhipsa Mohapatra.
The entire event reflected the spirit of patriotism and saw active participation from the campus community, as students, alumni, faculty members, staff, and their families participated in the vibrant celebrations.
