# Post Graduate Programme in Public Policy and Management
{IMG /sites/default/files/inline-images/PGPPM_1.jpg}
The Post Graduate Programme in Public Policy and Management (PGPPM) is 14-month post graduate programme offered by IIMB leading to a Masters of Management Studies (Public Policy) degree. PGPPM is aimed at enhancing efficiency, leadership and management skills among policy makers and administrators. PGPPM was introduced in 2001 along with the establishment of the Centre for Public Policy (CPP), with initial support from the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Government of India (GoI) and the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP). PGPPM provides a unique ecosystem for its participants, where research, practice, and leadership skills are brought to bear on public policy and management contexts.
