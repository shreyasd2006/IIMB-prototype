# IIT Kanpur and IIM Bangalore’s NSRCEL partner to strengthen India’s deep-tech and defence ecosystem
The partnership will enable joint incubation and acceleration initiatives, co-incubation opportunities, knowledge exchange and stronger connections with industry and the startup ecosystem
8 October 2026, Kanpur: In a first-of-its-kind collaboration bringing together India’s technology and business education ecosystems, the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (IIT Kanpur) and IIM Bangalore’s startup incubator NSRCEL have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to accelerate deep-tech startups and help translate emerging technologies into market-ready ventures.
The strategic collaboration builds on IIT Kanpur’s strengths in science, engineering, research and technology development, supported by its state-of-the-art R&D infrastructure. The institute’s Startup Incubation and Innovation Centre (SIIC) serves as its technology business incubator, supporting the development and commercialisation of technology-driven ventures. Within this ecosystem, SCALE is IIT Kanpur’s venture development initiative, and SCALE Catapult is its deep-tech startup accelerator. The partnership with NSRCEL brings together IIT Kanpur’s technical innovation expertise and infrastructure with the Centre’s strengths in startup incubation, industry partnerships and market access. It will initially support SCALE Catapult, with its first cohort focused on defence, aerospace and allied dual-use technologies, while establishing a broader framework for future collaboration in incubation, acceleration and entrepreneurship.
At the heart of this collaboration is the shared objective of helping deep-tech innovations move beyond technical validation towards commercially viable, real-world deployment. SCALE Catapult is designed to bridge this gap by combining access to technical expertise and advanced R&D capabilities with structured customer discovery, market validation and venture-building support. The programme enables startups to further strengthen and validate their technologies while identifying market opportunities, refining their business models and building pathways to commercialisation and scale.
The MoU was signed at IIT Kanpur by Prof. Jayandharan Giridhara Rao, Dean of Research and Development, IIT Kanpur, and Ms. Rasika Prashant, CEO, NSRCEL, IIM Bangalore.
Speaking on the occasion, Prof. Jayandharan Giridhara Rao, Dean of Research and Development, IIT Kanpur, said: “IIT Kanpur has a strong pipeline of deep-tech innovation, and creating pathways for these technologies to reach the market is an important institutional priority. Our collaboration with IIM Bangalore’s NSRCEL brings together complementary strengths in technology and entrepreneurship. We see this as an important step towards building a stronger ecosystem for technology-led ventures emerging from academic and research institutions.”
Ms. Rasika Prashant, CEO, NSRCEL, IIM Bangalore, said: “This one-of-a-kind partnership is a vital nation-building strategy that aligns directly with our country's agenda of building robust defence and aerospace capabilities. By combining our complementary expertise, IIT Kanpur’s deep tech capabilities and NSRCEL, IIM Bangalore’s experience in scaling market-ready ventures, this partnership can create meaningful pathways to identify and nurture the next generation of deeptech innovators in strategic sectors of national importance.”
Under the collaboration, the two institutions will explore joint incubation and acceleration programmes, co-incubation opportunities for startups, exchange of entrepreneurial best practices, joint ecosystem activities, and engagement with industry, investors and other stakeholders. The partnership is intended to create opportunities for startups to draw on the complementary resources and networks of both institutions.
The signing brought together the teams driving the collaboration. From IIT Kanpur, Prof. Raja Angamuthu, Professor-in-charge, IOTA, IIT Kanpur; Mr. Anurag Singh, SCALE Architect and Managing Partner, Dhanavritti Ventures; Dr. Poorna Roy, Program Director, SCALE; Dr. Ashutosh Agnihotri, CEO, SIIC and Mr. Piyush Mishra, COO, SIIC, joined Prof. Jayandharan Giridhara Rao. The NSRCEL team included Mr. Pradhyanand Kumbhare, AVP, NSRCEL, and Mr. Pranav Chaturvedi, alongside Ms. Rasika Prashant.
At a time when technological capabilities are increasingly critical to national competitiveness, the partnership of IIT Kanpur and IIM Bangalore’s NSRCEL aims to strengthen India's ability to translate research and innovation into globally competitive deep-tech enterprises.
Through SCALE Catapult and future collaborative initiatives, the partnership seeks to contribute to a more robust deep-tech ecosystem—supporting technology self-reliance, strategic capability and innovation-led national development, while enabling Indian technologies and entrepreneurs to create impact at scale.
