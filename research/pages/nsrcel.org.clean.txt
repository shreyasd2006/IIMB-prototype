At NSRCEL, we believe founders are the driving force behind India’s tech future. But scaling something extraordinary isn’t easy. The startup journey is as demanding as it is rewarding and that’s where we come in. Established with the support and vision of Mr. N. S. Raghavan, co-founder of Infosys, NSRCEL was created to nurture the next generation of entrepreneurs and innovation. For 25 years, we’ve been more than just an incubator-we’re a launchpad for tech innovation, a network of industry leaders, and a powerhouse of resources designed to help entrepreneurs survive, scale, and thrive. Because when startups succeed, India leads the way.

## Measurable Impact
Support fintech startups creating real solutions in access to financial products like credit, insurance, and wealth management, with outcomes that are trackable, reportable, and meaningful.
{IMG https://nsrcel.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/01.png}

Support fintech ventures solving India-first problems, from tier 2/3 financial literacy to cluster-based finance with the potential to become blueprints for emerging markets worldwide.
{IMG https://nsrcel.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/02.png}
