# Prof. Anirudh Dhawan appointed Subject Editor at Journal of International Financial Markets, Institutions and Money
{IMG /sites/default/files/2026-09/Prof.%20Anirudh%20Dhawan.JPG}
The international finance journal is recognized in the ABDC A and ABS 3 categories
25 September, 2026, Bengaluru : Prof. [https://www.iimb.ac.in/user/231/anirudh-dhawan] Anirudh Dhawan , faculty in the Finance & Accounting area at IIMB, has been appointed Subject Editor at the Journal of International Financial Markets, Institutions and Money (JIFMIM), a leading international peer-reviewed journal.
Prof. Dhawan’s research interests at IIMB span behavioural finance, financial markets and experimental finance, with his editorial appointment bringing this expertise to the journal’s evaluation and development of research in the field.
JIFMIM publishes rigorous, original research on the international dimensions of financial markets, institutions and monetary systems, including the evolving dynamics of foreign-currency-denominated transactions and their implications for financial markets.
The journal is ranked A in the Australian Business Deans Council (ABDC) Journal Quality List and 3 in the Academic Journal Guide (ABS).
