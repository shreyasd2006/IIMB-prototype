# IIM Bangalore opens applications for BSc (Hons) programmes in Data Sciences and Economics for 2027
{IMG /sites/default/files/2026-08/4U4A0186%20copy.jpg}
The application window is open from 17th August to 17th October 2026
18 August, 2026, Bengaluru : IIM Bangalore has opened admissions for the second cohort of its [https://ug.iimb.ac.in/] Undergraduate Programmes , with applications invited for the 2027 batch of its four-year, full-time residential BSc (Hons) in Data Sciences and BSc (Hons) in Economics programmes. The application window is open from 17th August to 17th October 2026, 5:00 pm IST.
Applications can be submitted through the UG admissions portal: [https://cdn.digialm.com/EForms/configuredHtml/1345/101967/Registration.html] https://cdn.digialm.com/EForms/configuredHtml/1345/101967/Registration.html
The programmes will admit 80 students in total, with 40 seats in each programme, enabling focused learning, close faculty interaction and personalized academic engagement within IIM Bangalore’s campus environment.
Students enrolled in the BSc (Hons) in Data Sciences will pursue minors in Economics and Business Studies, while students in the BSc (Hons) in Economics will pursue minors in Data Science and Business Studies. The programmes also provide opportunities for faculty-guided research, industry projects, internships and corporate interactions, along with access to IIM Bangalore’s wider alumni and professional community.
The four-year residential programmes were launched by IIM Bangalore last year with the aim of extending its legacy of academic excellence in postgraduate education to the undergraduate space. They are designed to develop analytical thinking, problem-solving and decision-making capabilities. The interdisciplinary approach equips students to engage with complex economic, technological and business challenges and develop evidence-based approaches to decision-making and development.
Admission and eligibility
Candidates must be 20 years of age or younger as on 1 August 2027 and must have passed Class XII or an equivalent examination from a recognized board. Candidates must have studied Mathematics in both Classes XI and XII and secured at least 60% marks in Mathematics in Class X.
Admission will be based on performance in the IIM Bangalore UG Admissions Test, followed by a personal interview for shortlisted candidates.
More information on the programmes, eligibility and admissions process is available [https://ug.iimb.ac.in/ug-admissions-2027/] HERE .
Important dates
Applications open: 17th August 2026
Application deadline: 17th October 2026, 5:00 pm IST
UG Admissions Test: 15th November 2026 (tentative)
Interview dates: 2nd and 3rd week of January 2027 (tentative)
Offers & waitlist: 3rd week of February 2027 (tentative)
For queries, contact [mailto:ugadmissions@iimb.ac.in] ugadmissions@iimb.ac.in or call 080-2699 3940/3941.
