# CSITM invites paper submissions for 'The India Lens: Building Theory from Indian Phenomena', a Scholarly Advancement Mentorship Workshop
Two-day workshop with the AIS India Chapter, led by Prof. Abhay Mishra, will be held on 1 and 2 December 2026
8 October, 2026, October: The [https://www.iimb.ac.in/centres-of-excellence/csitm] Centre for Software and IT Management (CSITM) at IIM Bangalore, in collaboration with the Association for Information Systems (AIS) India Chapter, has announced a two-day Scholarly Advancement Mentorship Workshop titled 'The India Lens: Building Theory from Indian Phenomena'. The workshop will be held on 1 and 2 December 2026 at the Institute campus. The last date for paper submission is 30 October 2026.
Fill this form, to apply now: [https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeGa_rmr_szMX1DngT_yzi6Jm9PPNZkEyPXqFack-cGWYlInw/viewform] The India Lens: Building Theory from Indian Phenomena
The workshop is an initiative to nurture scholars, strengthen research capabilities and advance high-quality research towards publication. Through a series of scholarly initiatives and events, CSITM seeks to foster a vibrant research ecosystem in India by offering scholars focused training, mentoring and opportunities for meaningful scholarly engagement.
Call for papers
CSITM and the AIS India Chapter invite submissions that address a research problem, idea or phenomenon rooted in India and employ a rigorous research methodology, such as a multimethod design. Authors must submit a full paper of 7,000 to 8,000 words, as the workshop is designed to advance promising research towards publication.
A selection committee will review all submissions and shortlist five papers for the closed-group workshop on the IIM Bangalore campus. Prof. Abhay Mishra will work closely with the selected authors, providing intensive, hands-on guidance to strengthen their manuscripts and take them towards publication in leading journals.
About Prof. Abhay Mishra
Prof. Abhay Mishra is Professor of Information Systems and David D. Glass Endowed Chair at the Sam M. Walton College of Business, University of Arkansas. His research examines the economic, organisational, individual and societal effects of digital transformation, with interests in digital health, artificial intelligence, platforms and the sharing economy, and the role of technology in national development.
His work has appeared in Information Systems Research , MIS Quarterly , Management Science , Production and Operations Management and the Journal of Operations Management , among others. He has been an Associate Editor at Information Systems Research since 2023 and received its Outstanding AE Award in 2024. He earlier served as an Associate Editor at MIS Quarterly . He is committed to mentoring doctoral students and early-career faculty.
Registration Fee (for selected participants)
Doctoral scholars: ₹6,000
Faculty and professionals: ₹10,000
Fees are exclusive of GST.
How to apply
Interested authors may apply through [https://forms.gle/PZ7PVxXy2LiKaz1g6] this form by 30 October 2026. Shortlisted authors will be informed by 15 November 2026 for registration. For queries, please write to [mailto:csitm@iimb.ac.in] csitm@iimb.ac.in .
