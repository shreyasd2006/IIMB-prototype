# IIMB-Real Estate Research Initiative (RERI)
The Indian Institute of Management Bangalore’s Real Estate Research Initiative (IIMB-RERI) is a one-of-its-kind Initiative in the country with an aspiration to be the world-class Centre of Excellence that is fully dedicated to the Indian real estate (RE) and allied sectors. Currently, it’s the only such initiative in the country dedicatedly conducting scientific and inter-disciplinary research, driving policy, teaching the nuances and engaging with all stakeholders across the Real Estate (RE) spectrum. Other activities include community outreach and engagement with the general public.
