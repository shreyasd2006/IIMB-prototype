# Four PGP alumni feature on ET’s ‘40 Under Forty’ list 2026
{IMG /sites/default/files/2026-09/parul-karan-mohit_piyush.jpg}
Parul Bajaj, Karan Dhall, Mohit Arora and Piyush Goswami recognized among young business leaders shaping India’s corporate landscape
16 September, 2026, Bengaluru : Four IIM Bangalore alumni of the [https://www.iimb.ac.in/programmes/pgp] Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) have been named to The Economic Times 40 Under Forty, a list that recognizes outstanding young leaders across India’s business landscape under the age of forty.
Selected by a distinguished jury, the winners are drawn from professionals who demonstrate exceptional expertise, innovative thinking and a commitment to driving meaningful change within their organizations and the wider industry.
The alumni featured on the list are:
PGP 2010 Parul Bajaj, Managing Director & Partner, Boston Consulting Group
PGP 2011 Karan Dhall, Partner, Kearney India
PGP 2012 Mohit Arora, Managing Director, Blackstone
PGP 2014 Piyush Goswami, Partner, Bain & Company
At Boston Consulting Group, Parul Bajaj leads the Marketing, Sales & Pricing practice in India, working on profitable growth acceleration across the consumer and industrial goods sectors. Her work spans consumer durables, electricals, FMCG, fashion and luxury, and automotive, with an emphasis on translating strategy into measurable on-ground impact.
Karan Dhall , Partner in Kearney India’s Consumer Retail practice, has built his career at the firm across roles ranging from senior business analyst and associate to manager and partner. His work in retail is shaped by what he describes as the sector’s “day-to-day hustle and bustle” and the opportunity to create immediate impact on the ground.
Mohit Arora is a Managing Director in Blackstone’s Real Estate Group. Since joining the firm in 2013, he has been involved in several of Blackstone’s investments in India, including Indiabulls, the Salarpuria Office Portfolio and Panchshil Office Parks.
Piyush Goswami is a member of Bain's Technology & Cloud Services practice, where he advises IT services and software/SaaS companies across the firm’s Mumbai and New York offices. His work has involved full potential strategy, organization design, and performance improvement, with added experience at the intersection of private equity and technology, as well as healthcare and technology.
Their recognition spans consulting, strategy and investment domains, and reflects the diverse leadership trajectories of IIM Bangalore’s alumni across India’s corporate sector.
