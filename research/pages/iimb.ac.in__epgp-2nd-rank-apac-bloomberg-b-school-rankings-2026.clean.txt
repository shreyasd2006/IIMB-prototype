# EPGP clinches #2 rank in APAC: Bloomberg B-School Rankings 2026
{IMG /sites/default/files/2026-09/DSC06747.JPG}
The one-year residential MBA holds onto #1 spot in India for the fifth consecutive year
15 September, 2026, Bengaluru : The [https://www.iimb.ac.in/programmes/epgp?Ref=undefined] Executive Post Graduate Programme in Management (EPGP) , IIM Bangalore’s one-year residential MBA for experienced professionals, has been ranked #2 in the Asia-Pacific region in the Bloomberg Businessweek Best Business-Schools Survey 2026, moving up from #3 in 2025. EPGP has also retained its position as the No. 1 MBA programme in India for the fifth consecutive year.
This is the sixth cycle of the Bloomberg Businessweek survey in which EPGP has been represented. IIMB’s overall score rose from 71.7 in 2025 to 72.6 in 2026, with particularly strong gains in learning, networking and entrepreneurship.
Under the Learning Index , the programme’s score increased from 89.3 to 90.4, reflecting the quality and range of instruction, the connection between classroom learning and real business situations, and the emphasis on strategic thinking, problem-solving, innovation and collaborative learning. The Networking Index rose from 84.7 to 87.7, capturing the value of interactions among students and alumni, career support, employer recognition and the breadth of the IIMB network. The largest improvement came in the Entrepreneurship Index , which increased from 82.1 to 86.2, with students and alumni reporting on the seriousness with which entrepreneurship is treated as a career path and the quality of preparation for building new ventures. This year, the programme earned its highest-ever scores in all three indices. Under the Compensation Index , EPGP once again placed 2nd in the APAC region.
“The strongest gains this year have come in learning, networking and entrepreneurship, which are central to what EPGP is trying to do for experienced professionals. The improvement across these areas suggests that the value of the programme has extended well beyond the classroom, and into the networks, insights and cross-industry perspectives that these candidates will be taking back into their careers”, Prof. [https://www.iimb.ac.in/user/70/u-dinesh-kumar] U Dinesh Kumar , Director-in-charge, IIM Bangalore.
The Bloomberg Businessweek rankings draw on feedback from graduating students, recent alumni and corporate recruiters, covering dimensions of the business-school experience that range from classroom learning and career outcomes to alumni networks and employer perceptions.
The EPGP is designed for professionals who come with experience in the workplace and are looking to make a significant shift in the scale or direction of their careers. The one-year format, which makes the programme deliberately intensive, brings together professionals from various industries and functions and places them in an environment that is global in its orientation, dynamic in its approach to management education, and hands-on in the way it engages with complex business problems.
