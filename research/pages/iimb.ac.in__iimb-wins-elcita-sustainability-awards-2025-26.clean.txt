# IIMB wins gold along with three Excellence Awards at ELCITA Sustainability Awards 2025–26
{IMG /sites/default/files/2026-09/DSC03112.JPG.jpeg}
The Institute is being recognised for its leadership in water conservation, biodiversity, and community service
18 August, 2026, Bangalore: IIM Bangalore has been conferred the ELCITA Gold Award, its highest honour, in addition to three Awards for Excellence in Water, Biodiversity, and Social Welfare and Community Services, at the ELCITA Sustainability Awards 2025–26, marking a step up from the Institute's recognition the previous year which had received silver awards and reaffirming IIMB's holistic commitment to sustainability and community engagement.
Instituted by the Electronics City Industrial Township Authority (ELCITA), the awards celebrate organizations that lead with responsible environmental action, recognising initiatives across water conservation, wastewater management, rainwater harvesting, solid and organic waste management, energy conservation and renewable energy, air quality improvement, green infrastructure, biodiversity preservation, sustainability reporting, and carbon neutrality. IIMB's Gold Award places it among the top-recognised educational and research institutions for the depth and impact of its environmental stewardship.
Prof. [https://www.iimb.ac.in/user/136/haritha-saranga] Haritha Saranga , Chairperson, IIMB Sustainability Taskforce, thanked all staff members for their continued commitment to the Institute's sustainability journey, noting that this recognition reflects the collective effort of everyone who works, day in and day out, to build a greener campus.
{IMG /sites/default/files/inline-images/DSC02901.JPG}
This achievement reflects the sustained effort of IIMB's Sustainability Taskforce, Hostel, the Horticulture and Estate department managers, staff and workers, and the wider IIMB community, whose everyday work, from tending the campus's green cover to managing water and waste systems, has been central to this recognition. As the Institute builds on this momentum, it reaffirms its shared commitment to a greener, more inclusive campus.
