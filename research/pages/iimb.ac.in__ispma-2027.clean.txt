# Centre for Software and IT Management (CSITM) to host the 9th India Software Product Management Summit
Three-day summit in collaboration with the International Software Product Management Association to explore product management in the age of Agentic AI
02 September, 2026, Bangalore: The [https://www.iimb.ac.in/centres-of-excellence/csitm] Centre for Software and IT Management (CSITM) at IIM Bangalore, in collaboration with the International Software Product Management Association (ISPMA), will host the 9th India Software Product Management Summit from 11–13 February 2027 at the IIMB campus. The theme of the conference is ‘Redefining Product Management for the Agentic Era: Delivering Enterprise-Scale ROAI (Return on AI).’
The three-day summit will bring together academics, software product management practitioners, industry experts, researchers, and students to explore the evolving landscape of software product management in the age of Artificial Intelligence. It will offer a platform for participants to exchange ideas, share industry experience, present research, and discuss the emerging practices and challenges shaping the future of software products.
The conference will be chaired by Prof . Sowmya Narayanan Sadagopan , Founding Director, IIIT Bangalore, and Chairman, ISPMA India, and co-chaired by Mr. Venkatesh Mahadevan, FBCS, Fellow, ISPMA India, and Dr. Tathagat Varma, author of Theory of Cognitive Chasms , who also serves as Industry Chair for the summit. The summit leadership team further includes Prof. Merlin Nandy, Associate Professor, IIM Sambalpur, as Academic Chair, and Prof. [https://www.iimb.ac.in/user/268/mayank-kumar] Mayank Kumar, Chairperson, CSITM, IIM Bangalore, as Co-host.
The programme will feature workshops, research paper presentations, panel discussions, and talks by industry practitioners, spanning contemporary themes at the intersection of software product management, technology, business, and AI. Discussions will examine how AI is reshaping product strategy, product development, customer experience, product leadership, and innovation, as well as the broader responsibilities of software product managers in an increasingly AI-driven world.
Three dedicated tracks will allow participants to engage with the subject from different vantage points. The Student Track will encourage emerging talent to learn from experienced practitioners and academics. The Industry Track will showcase practical insights, case studies, and experiences from organisations building and managing software products. The Research Track will provide a forum for presenting and discussing new research and academic perspectives in software product management.
A key highlight of the summit will be the SPM Excellence Awards, recognising outstanding software products across categories that reflect exemplary product thinking, innovation, execution, and impact — celebrating the teams shaping the software product ecosystem.
The 9th India Software Product Management Summit aims to be a vibrant forum for learning, collaboration, knowledge exchange, and networking, bringing together academic and practitioner communities to shape the future of software product management in the AI era.
Further details on the programme, participation, submission guidelines, and registration will be updated on the ISPMA India website: [https://ispma.org/india/] https://ispma.org/india/
