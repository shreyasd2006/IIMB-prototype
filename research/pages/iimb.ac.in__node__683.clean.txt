# Centre for Teaching & Learning to host seventh cohort of Nurturing Future Leadership Programme (NFLP)
The five-day residential programme for HEI faculty members will be held from 2-6 November 2026
22 September, 2026, Bengaluru : The [https://www.iimb.ac.in/centres-of-excellence/ctl] Centre for Teaching and Learning (CTL) at IIMB will host the seventh cohort of the Nurturing Future Leadership Programme (NFLP) from 2nd to 6th November 2026. Supported by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, the five-day residential programme is designed for faculty members in higher educational institutions who are aspiring to take on academic leadership roles.
The programme is being offered in the context of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, which emphasizes capacity building among faculty members of higher educational institutions and the development of future academic leaders.
Eligible participants include full-time faculty members of centrally funded institutes, state public universities/institutes and private higher educational institutions, with the number of seats determined as per MMTTP norms. Faculty members from recognized institutions must be nominated by their Director, Vice Chancellor or Head of Institution and submit the nomination letter through the [https://mmc.ugc.ac.in/] Malaviya Mission Teacher Training Programme (MMTTC) portal .
The NFLP aims to help faculty members in the early stages of their careers develop the capabilities needed to emerge as effective academic leaders. The programme will focus on areas including leadership and institution building, leadership and teamwork, strategic thinking and planning, decision-making, building centres of excellence in academia, fostering equity and inclusivity, institutional legal aspects, entrepreneurial ecosystems in higher education institutions, digital learning capacity and communicating for impact.
The sessions will be led by senior and eminent faculty members of IIM Bangalore and will combine lectures, video-based lectures, in-class activities and case discussions. Five sessions will be conducted each day, with each session lasting 75 minutes. Participants who successfully complete the programme will receive a certificate of participation, with attendance across all five days being mandatory.
The programme will be held at IIM Bangalore’s Bannerghatta Road campus. For more details, view the [/sites/default/files/inline-files/Brochure_NFLP_November-2026.pdf] programme brochure .
For further information, reach out to the Centre for Teaching and Learning at [mailto:ctl@iimb.ac.in] ctl@iimb.ac.in or call 080-26993852.
