Empowering Boards for a Sustainable Future. Inspiring Directors to Lead with Purpose.
The Centre for Corporate Governance & Sustainability (CCGS) at IIM Bangalore actively engages with directors, Boards, business leaders, and aspirants to strengthen governance practices, embed sustainability into decision-making, and expand ethical leadership in boardrooms both within India and beyond. We seek to turn responsibility into results.
