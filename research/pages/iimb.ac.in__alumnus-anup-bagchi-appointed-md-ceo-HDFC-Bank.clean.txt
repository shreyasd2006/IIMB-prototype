# IIMB alumnus Anup Bagchi appointed MD & CEO of HDFC Bank
{IMG /sites/default/files/2026-10/images.jpeg}
PGP 1992 alum brings over three decades of financial services experience to the helm of the private sector bank
2 October, 2026, Bengaluru : Anup Bagchi, PGP 1992 alumnus, has been appointed as the Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of HDFC Bank for a three-year term, following approval from the Reserve Bank of India.
Mr. Bagchi brings over three decades of experience in financial services, with a career spanning banking, capital markets, wealth management, insurance and digital financial services. He has been associated with the ICICI Group since 1992 and has held several senior leadership positions, including CEO & MD of ICICI Securities, Executive Director of ICICI Bank, and MD & CEO of ICICI Prudential Life Insurance.
As Executive Director of ICICI Bank from 2017 to 2023, he oversaw key businesses across retail, business and rural banking, and later wholesale banking. His career has been marked by leadership across diverse segments of the financial services industry and a strong focus on business building and strategic growth.
His appointment as the first external candidate to lead HDFC Bank marks a significant milestone in the institution’s leadership journey.
