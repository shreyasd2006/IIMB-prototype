# IIM Bangalore to host AIB South Asia Chapter & AIB Sustainability Shared Interest Group paper development workshop
The workshop is themed ‘Sustainability in International Business: A Paper Development Workshop for South Asia Scholars’
29 September, 2026, Bangalore: The Academy of International Business (AIB) South Asia Chapter and the AIB Sustainability Shared Interest Group (SIG) will host a Paper Development Workshop (PDW) on ‘Sustainability in International Business: A Paper Development Workshop for South Asia Scholars’ at IIM Bangalore on 12 December 2026. The workshop is chaired by Prof. Sandeep Yadav , Strategy area, IIM Bangalore and Officer of AIB sustainability SIG.
Workshop Overview
The Professional Development Workshop (PDW) is designed to give scholars developmental feedback on sustainability-related International Business (IB) research. It offers a supportive environment in which participants receive constructive comments from experienced researchers, helping them strengthen their manuscripts for submission to leading IB journals such as the Journal of International Business Studies, Journal of World Business, Global Strategy Journal and Journal of International Management.
Sustainability has become a central theme in IB research, spanning climate change, ESG, sustainable global value chains, responsible internationalization, the circular economy, social inclusion, energy transition, biodiversity and the Sustainable Development Goals. South Asia is an important setting for such work because of its rapidly growing economies, institutional diversity, environmental vulnerabilities, expanding multinational activity and increasing integration into global value chains.
Many scholars, particularly doctoral students and early-career researchers, have limited opportunities to receive detailed feedback before submitting to journals. The workshop addresses this gap through a structured mentoring environment in which participants receive in-depth comments from senior scholars with expertise in sustainability and International Business.
Who Should Apply
The workshop welcomes submissions from doctoral students and faculty researching sustainability-related IB topics. Participants attending the ISC Conference and the AIB South Asia Chapter Conference may also submit the same paper to this PDW. Both conceptual and empirical papers at various stages of development are welcome.
Please note: Valid AIB Membership at the time of the event is required.
Relevant topics include, but are not limited to, climate change and international business, ESG and corporate sustainability, sustainable global value chains, and responsible internationalization. They also include emerging market multinationals and sustainability, sustainable innovation, the circular economy, green supply chains, and sustainable finance. Further themes are institutional environments and sustainability, informality and sustainable development, inclusive business models, sustainable entrepreneurship, and digital technologies for sustainability.
Workshop Format
The workshop will follow a roundtable format in which participants are organised into small groups, each with one mentor and four to five paper presenters. Group composition may change depending on the number of submissions and mentors.
Each paper will receive approximately 25 to 30 minutes of discussion. The author will begin with a brief presentation of five to seven minutes, followed by detailed developmental comments from the mentor for 12 to 15 minutes. The session will close with an interactive discussion with participants for eight to ten minutes.
Submission Process
Applicants are required to submit an extended abstract of two to three pages or a full working paper, along with a short statement describing the stage of the research and the specific feedback they seek.
Note : Submissions should be emailed to sandeep.yadav@iimb.ac.in and sustainabilitysig@aib.world , with the subject line "Submission for AIB Sustainability SIG PDW".
Submissions will be evaluated on their relevance to sustainability and International Business, originality, potential academic contribution, and potential for constructive development through the workshop. Preference will be given to papers that would benefit most from developmental feedback.
