# Prof. Gopal Das joins Editorial Review Board of Journal of Consumer Psychology
{IMG /sites/default/files/2026-10/gopal-das_0%20copy.jpg}
JCP is a Financial Times 50 (FT50) journal
5 October, 2026, Bengaluru : Prof. [https://www.iimb.ac.in/user/64/gopal-das] Gopal Das , faculty in the Marketing Area at IIM Bangalore, has accepted an invitation to join the Editorial Review Board (ERB) of the Journal of Consumer Psychology (JCP), a Financial Times 50 (FT50) journal.
The Journal of Consumer Psychology publishes research on the psychology of consumer behaviour, including consumer judgment and decision-making, information processing, and affective, cognitive, and motivational processes.
Prof. Das's research interests include consumer emotion, motivation, scarcity, information processing, gift-giving, consumer receptivity to artificial intelligence, and retailing. He teaches Marketing Management, Marketing Research, Consumer Behaviour, and Experimental Design at IIM Bangalore.
