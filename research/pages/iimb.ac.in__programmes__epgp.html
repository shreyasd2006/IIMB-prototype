Executive Post Graduate Programme in Management: Empowering the Next Generation of Leaders

The Executive Post Graduate Programme in Management (EPGP) leading to

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

About the Programme

The fulltime Executive Post Graduate Programme in Management (EPGP) is an intensive one-year residential MBA for professionals with minimum 5 years of work experience. It enhances the skills and capabilities of the participants, and prepares them for senior management roles. A diverse cohort, rich in experience, is a key strength of the programme.

Download EPGP Brochure

Why EPGP

Global Footprint

Innovative in design and boasting an International Immersion module, this programme equips you to excel in India and abroad.

Electives

With over 100+ electives to choose from, the programme offers customised learning in emerging fields, aligned with career goals.

Location

Situated in India’s tech and start-up hub, where Fortune 500 companies abound, IIM Bangalore offers exciting opportunities for networking and collaboration. The city’s weather and vibe are a bonus!

World-class Faculty

Academics who combine research rigour with industry experience, the faculty body at IIM Bangalore revels in challenging bright minds.

Global Networks

As a valued member of the Global Network for Advanced Management (GNAM), comprising 32 top B-schools, IIM Bangalore offers you the opportunity to step into classrooms across the world and engage with vision-driven professionals.

Vibrant Peer Cohort

Professionals from diverse sectors and competitive portfolios arrive with a spirit of inquiry and this is sure to make the programme a transformative experience.

Message from the Chairperson

Professor Amar Sapra

The programme’s strength lies in its rigorous and flexible curriculum, combining core courses with over 100 electives to choose from. This approach gives students a solid understanding of global business practices, strategic management principles, and insights into diverse industries. Students benefit immensely from IIM Bangalore's distinguished faculty, who blend conceptual knowledge with practical applications.

Another distinguishing feature of the programme is the two-week international immersion. IIMB was a pioneer in India in including such a module in its curriculum. The immersion is ranked first in India and seventh worldwide, according to the Financial Times in 2024. Students not only learn about international business practices but also experience personal growth by observing and immersing themselves in different cultures.

Now in its 15th year, the programme has over one thousand graduates, many of whom hold senior leadership positions worldwide in industries such as IT, banking, consulting, FMCG, and retail. The programme’s high global rankings from Financial Times, QS, BusinessWeek, and other agencies reflect its top-notch curriculum, faculty, and students. I am proud of our graduates, whose success reflects their talent and the holistic development they received at IIM Bangalore.

I invite you to apply to IIM Bangalore.

We seek candidates who…

  • Thrive in a rigorous academic environment
  • Possess strong communication skills
  • Have led teams and contributed to projects that impact the organisation’s bottom line

FAQs

General Programme Information

1.  How is EPGP different from PGP?

First, the similarities. Both are fulltime on-campus programmes, leading to the grant of an MBA degree. Now, the key difference: the EPGP is a one-year programme designed specifically for experienced professionals. It builds on their existing experience to prepare them for senior management positions. The PGP is a two-year programme that accepts candidates with little or no work experience.

2.  What degree will I get at the end of the programme?

An MBA degree that is globally valued.

3.  Will I get alumni status?

Yes.

4.  Is this programme offered in distance-learning mode?

No.

5.  Is the International Immersion component optional?

No, it is compulsory.

Application Process

1. When does the application process commence?

Round 1: Jun 15, 2026

Round 2: Aug 19, 2026

You can apply during any of the two rounds.

2. When is the last date to apply?

Round 1: Aug 17, 2026 (5:00 p.m. IST)

Round 2: Nov 25, 2026 (5:00 p.m. IST)

3. How does one apply to the programme?

Please visit the IIMB website click https://www.iimb.ac.in/epgp-admissions

4.  When will the interviews be conducted?

Round 1: September 05 & 06, 2026

Round 2: December 12 & 13, 2026

Admission Process

1.  What are the admission criteria?

  • Academic background
  • Work experience
  • Valid GMAT/GRE score
  • Statement of purpose
  • Two reference letters (Online)
  • Interview and written test

2.  Is there a separate admission process for sponsored candidates?

No

3.  Is there any relaxation of admission criteria for PhD holders?

No

4.  Is GMAT/GRE compulsory for admission?

Yes, a valid GMAT/GRE is mandatory. You must mention your GMAT or GRE score when submitting your application.

5.  Is CAT considered for admission?

No

6. Is online GMAT/GRE score accepted?

No, not for the batch of 2027-28.

7.  What is the average GMAT score?

There is no minimum GMAT score. It is relative to the applicant pool.

8.  What is the validity period for the GMAT/GRE score?

Within 5 years as on the date of application submission in each round.

9.  What is the code for the programme to take the GMAT/GRE?

It is Q9H-KK-84 for GMAT, and 1675 for GRE. Use this code to have your score reported directly to IIMB. This requirement is mandatory.

10.  Can work experience be a replacement for GMAT/GRE score?

No

11.  I am a US citizen. Do I qualify to apply?

Yes

12.  What do you mean by ‘Proficiency in English’? How do you judge it?

Indian nationals need not take the TOEFL. However, TOEFL is required of applicants who have studied in countries

where the language of instruction is not English.

13. By when should I have five years of work experience?

Before 31 March 2027.

14.  Can the reference letters be sent by e-mail?

No. You must provide updated contact information of your referees in the online application. They, in turn, must submit the reference letters directly to IIMB online. Your referee will receive an email containing the link to submit the reference. A passcode will also be sent, on a separate email, to the referee. The referees must submit the references online using the reference link provided on or before the last date of application.

15.  By when should reference letters reach IIMB?

The reference section is a part of your online application. Reference letters must be submitted by the referees before the application due date of the respective rounds.

Applications cannot be submitted until reference letters are received online. Since your referees may take time to

submit reference letters, please provide the contact information of the referees as soon as possible.

16. When can I expect to get the final admission offer?

Admission offers will be sent by email to successful candidates by 21 September 2026 for Round 1; 24 December 2026 for Round 2.

17.  I have submitted the reference details, but my referees have not received the link and passcode.

Please note that you will receive an email from IIMB from the id: epgpadm[at]iimb[dot]ac[dot]in. Please manage your spam folders and inform your referees about the same.

Interview Process

1. When will interviews be conducted?

Round 1: September 05 & 06, 2026; Round 2: December 12 & 13, 2026.

2. Where are the interviews conducted?

Interviews are conducted in Bangalore, New Delhi, Mumbai and Kolkata. Online interviews will be arranged for applicants who are outside India on the interview dates. Interviews for any/all rounds may be conducted online if there is a disruption due to extraneous factors.

3. What will be the mode of interview if I am overseas during the interview period?

Interviews may be conducted via video conference provided authorization and arrangements have been confirmed with the EPGP Admissions Office.
