# Centre for Capital Markets and Risk Management signs MoU with ACUMO Graylight
{IMG /sites/default/files/2026-09/DSC05966.JPG}
The collaboration on ACUMO Finathon brings financial literacy to 400+ schools nationwide, with national finals at IIM Bangalore
09 September, 2026, Bangalore: The [https://www.iimb.ac.in/ccmrm/] Centre for Capital Markets and Risk Management (CCMRM), IIM Bangalore, signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with ACUMO Graylight Learning Labs LLP on 08th September, 2026. The partnership deepens the two organisations' ongoing collaboration on the ACUMO Finathon, a nationwide school finance competition, and formalises ACUMO's role as CCMRM's technical and content partner.
India is adding retail investors faster than it is adding informed ones, and the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has been working to close that gap by building financial literacy at an earlier stage. Financial education introduced at age 30 functions as remediation; introduced at 16, it becomes foundational. CCMRM's collaboration with ACUMO is built around this premise: that informed investing begins well before students have money to invest.
This year's edition of the ACUMO Finathon is the largest yet, spanning 400+ schools nationwide and sponsored by Google Chromebooks. The national finals will be hosted at IIM Bangalore.
Under the terms of the MoU, ACUMO will serve as CCMRM's technical and content partner, developing course content built around Google Sheets, along with the assessments layered on top of it. The collaboration extends beyond the annual competition to include foundational finance courses, ongoing assessments, and learning journeys built on AI-enabled spreadsheets.
Speaking on the collaboration, Prof. [https://www.iimb.ac.in/user/78/m-jayadev] Jayadev M , Chairperson, CCMRM, IIM Bangalore, said that “through this collaboration with ACUMO, CCMRM aims to make practical finance education more accessible and engaging for learners. The integration of interactive spreadsheet-based learning with academic oversight from IIM Bangalore will help students build stronger financial understanding and apply concepts through hands-on learning.”
Faiz Azim, Co-founder, ACUMO, called it a special homecoming, collaborating with his alma mater on a mission close to his heart. He shared that IIM Bangalore, Prof. Jayadev, and Utanka Sarma, Head of Alumni Relations, were instrumental when ACUMO Finathon's inaugural edition came together, and that the team now looks forward to working together more deeply, delivering not just foundational finance courses but ongoing assessments and learning journeys built on AI-enabled spreadsheets.
