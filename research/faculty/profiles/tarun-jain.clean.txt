## Summary
Tarun Jain is a Professor in the Production and Operations Management Area at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore. His research interests include strategic sourcing, operational issues in cloud computing, management of digital technologies, smart city operations, urban mobility, and pricing issues in service supply chains. His work has been published in journals such as Production and Operations Management, IISE Transactions, Naval Research Logistics, Decision Sciences, Service Science, European Journal of Operational Research, IEEE Transactions on Engineering Management, Journal of Product Innovation Management, Journal of Operational Research Society, PLOS One, Journal of Revenue and Pricing Management and International Journal of Production Economics. Prior to joining IIMB, he served as Assistant Professor at the Indian Institute of Management Udaipur.

He teaches Operations Management, Revenue Management, and Operations Strategy at the postgraduate level, and Advanced Supply Chain Management at the doctoral level. He has also taught in executive education programmes for leading companies such as Asian Paints, KPMG, ICAI, Hindustan Unilever, Food Corporation of India, Tata Motors, GE HealthCare, GlobalFoundries, Pernod Ricard, and Applied Materials. He is the Programme Director for the Certificate Programme in Digital Operations and Supply Chain Management (2023–current) and the programme Digital Supply Chains: From Strategy to Execution.
