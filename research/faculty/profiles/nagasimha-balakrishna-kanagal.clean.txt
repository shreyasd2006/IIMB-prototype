## Accomplishments
[#home2] RESEARCH
[#profile2] TEACHING
[#profile4] CONSULTING
[#profile3] SERVICE
[#profile5] EDUCATION
Professor Kanagal’s research interests are in the area of strategic marketing. This includes Customer and Market Analysis, Competitive Marketing Strategies, Customer Relationship and Value Management. His papers have been accepted at international and national conferences and he has published in international and domestic journals.
He teaches principles of marketing, marketing research and strategic marketing.
Chairperson, Marketing Area: April 2017 to March 2019, IIM Bangalore
Chief Editor, IIMB Management Review: September 2011 to March 2018
Chairperson, Marketing Area: June 2005 to November 2009, IIM Bangalore
Chairperson, Library: June 2003 to June 2005, IIM Bangalore
He has over five years of organizational training and industry experience in leading technology organizations in the area of marketing.
PhD, University of Texas at Dallas, USA
MS, University of Texas at Dallas, USA
MA, University of Texas at Dallas, USA
PGDM, IIM Ahmedabad, India
BE, Bangalore University, India
