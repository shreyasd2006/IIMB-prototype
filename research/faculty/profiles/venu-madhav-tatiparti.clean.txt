## Summary
Venu Madhav Tatiparti completed his bachelors degree from Osmania University in 2013, and completed his chartered accountant qualification. He then pursued his MSc in Accounting and Finance from the London School of Economics and Political Science, London, UK. He graduated with a distinction. After his master’s, he joined the PhD program in management at the University of Texas at Dallas in August 2016.
