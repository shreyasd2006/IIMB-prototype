## Summary
Professor Ananth Krishnamurthy’s research focuses on stochastic modelling and optimization techniques and their applications in industry. Topics of interest include supply chain competitiveness, warehousing and logistics, biomanufacturing, and energy supply chains. His research has been supported by government agencies as well as industry. Dr. Krishnamurthy has served as the Director of the Center for Quick Response Manufacturing at the University of Wisconsin-Madison.
