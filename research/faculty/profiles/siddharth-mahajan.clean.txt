## Accomplishments
[#home2] RESEARCH
[#profile2] TEACHING
[#profile5] EDUCATION
Professor Mahajan’s research interests are Supply Chain Management and Retail Inventory Management. His other assignments include Modell's Sporting Goods: Productivity Analysis using DEA of a Chain of Sporting Goods and GM: Managing Popular Car Configurations for Custom Delivery.
His areas of specialisation are production and operations management.
PhD, The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania, USA
MA, The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania, USA
MS, Texas A&M University, USA
BTech, IIT Delhi, India
