## Summary
Raja Reddy Bujunoori is an Assistant Professor in the Finance and Accounting area at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB). His research focuses on information economics, financial intermediation, and monetary policy. He completed his doctoral studies in Finance at the Indian School of Business. He also holds a Post Graduate Diploma in Business Management from XLRI Jamshedpur and a bachelor’s degree from the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad.
