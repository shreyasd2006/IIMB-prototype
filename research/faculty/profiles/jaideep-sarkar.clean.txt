## Summary
Jaideep Sarkar is a Professor of Practice in the Entrepreneurship Area. He is a retired Indian Foreign Service officer with wide experience in Government, including tenures in the Prime Minister's Office, Ministry of Finance, Ministry of External Affairs and Indian missions abroad. His interests are in the areas of cross-cultural perspectives on innovation, emerging markets, country studies on Japan and Israel and innovations in public policy.
