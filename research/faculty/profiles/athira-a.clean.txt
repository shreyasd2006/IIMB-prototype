## Summary
Athira A is an Assistant Professor of Accounting at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB). Athira’s primary research interests include corporate tax management, principal-agent problems, audit quality, earnings management and changes in tax policies. Athira holds a PhD (FPM) in finance and accounting from IIM Kozhikode.
