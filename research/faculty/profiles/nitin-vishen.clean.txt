## Summary
I am an empirical researcher in Finance, focusing on Banking area. My current research projects examine the finances of low-income households, and the interaction between firms and banks. I am also interested in understanding how broader policy decisions impact these agents.
I completed my joint-doctoral studies in Finance from the Indian School of Business and Deakin University in 2023. I earned a Master’s degree in Finance from the University of Amsterdam, the Netherlands in 2016 and a Bachelor’s degree in Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi in 2011.
