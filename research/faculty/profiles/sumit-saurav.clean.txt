## Summary
Sumit Saurav is an Assistant Professor in the Finance and Accounting area of Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB). He has received his doctoral degree from the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad. He holds an MBA from Indian Institute of Management Visakhapatnam, and a B-Tech from the National Institute of Foundry and Forge Technology, Ranchi. He works in the field of asset pricing, options, and banking. His teaching interest lies in topics related to financial markets.
