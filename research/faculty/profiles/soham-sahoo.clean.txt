## Summary
Soham Sahoo is an Associate Professor in the Public Policy Area at IIM Bangalore. He holds a PhD in Quantitative Economics from the Indian Statistical Institute, Delhi. He has previously held research and teaching positions at the London School of Economics, Loughborough University, the University of Goettingen, and the University of Warwick. He is currently affiliated with the Institute of Labor Economics (IZA), the Global Action for Policy Initiative at Northeastern University, and J-PAL as an Invited Researcher.
Soham’s research and teaching interests lie at the intersection of economics and public policy, with a focus on education, labour, health, and political economy. His work has been published in leading academic journals and supported by several international research funders.
He currently serves as an Associate Editor at Economics of Education Review and as an Associate Data Editor at Economic Inquiry .
