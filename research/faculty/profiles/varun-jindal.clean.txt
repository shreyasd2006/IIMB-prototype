## Summary
Varun Jindal is currently working as an Associate Professor in the Finance and Accounting area at IIM Bangalore. He holds a PhD in Finance and Control from the triple crown-accredited Indian Institute of Management (IIM) Calcutta. Prior to pursuing PhD, he served in the industry for close to six years in various roles, including but not limited to, team management, strategic assistance to top management, software development, etc.
His research work has been published in the British Accounting Review , European Financial Management , the Journal of Corporate Finance , and the International Business Review . He has also co-authored a case for the IIM Calcutta Case Research Center. His research work has received best paper awards at various conferences, including the Research Symposium on Finance and Economics (RSFE) 2025 , the India Management Research Conference (IMRC) 2025 , and the Pan IIM World Management Conference 2017 . He is also a recipient of financial support of USD 3,750 for a policy paper under the NSE-NYU Stern Initiative on the Study of Indian Financial Markets 2018.
In addition to being recognized for his research work, he has also bagged several other awards and honours, including the Excellence in Teaching Awards at IIM Bangalore, the Distinguished Alumnus Award 2021 from the Panjab University Alumni Association, the Springer Best Discussant Award at the Emerging Markets Conference 2021, Satish K. Sehgal Doctoral Student Award for Excellence in Scholarship and Organizational Citizenship 2020 at IIM Calcutta, the Director’s Medal for the Best Outgoing Student of MBA Class of 2010, the CRISIL Young Thought Leader 2009 Runner-up Award, and the Knowledge Management Super Kruiser Award 2007 at Infosys Ltd. He has also had the honour of being invited to deliver The Last Lecture for the PGP/PGPBA Class of 2023 at IIM Bangalore.
Note: Prof. Jindal is currently not accepting applications for internships.
