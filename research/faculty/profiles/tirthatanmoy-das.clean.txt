## Summary
Dr. Tirthatanmoy Das is Professor in the Economics area. He is also a Research Fellow at IZA Institute of Labor Economics and a Fellow at the Global Labor Organization (GLO). Prior to joining IIM Bangalore in July 2017, He was an Assistant Professor of Economics at the University of Central Florida, USA from 2014 – 2017, and at Temple University, USA from 2012 – 2014. His research spans topics in Labour Economics, Econometrics, Health Economics and Behavioural Economics. He has published in leading peer-reviewed journals such as the Journal of Political Economy and the Journal of Econometrics . Dr. Das’s graduate teaching includes Masters (MA, MBA) and Doctoral level courses on Labour Economics, Econometrics and Mathematical Economics, whereas his undergraduate teaching includes courses on Microeconomics, Macroeconomics and Econometrics.
