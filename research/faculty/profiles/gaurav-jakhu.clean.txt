## Summary
Dr. Gaurav Jakhu is an Associate Professor in the Economics area at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore. His current research focuses on antitrust and regulatory issues in digital markets. He uses game-theoretic modelling to study strategic interactions among economic agents in digital markets and its impact on competition, innovation, and welfare.
He has a PhD in Economics from Indian Statistical Institute, Master’s in Economics from Delhi School of Economics, and a Bachelor of Arts in Economics from Shri Ram College of Commerce. Before his doctoral studies, he worked as an Economist at the Competition Commission of India, New Delhi.
