September 2013 Onwards: Extensive teaching experience as visiting faculty of Strategy at IIM Calcutta, IIM Ahmedabad, IIM Sirmaur, and IRMA
Extensive experience of executive education (MDPs and LDPs)
Work experience in Strategy and Organizations Consulting (Post-FPM)
