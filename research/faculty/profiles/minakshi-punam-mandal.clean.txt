## Summary
Minakshi Punam Mandal completed her PhD in Operations Research from ESSEC Business School in France in 2024. Her research is focused on sustainable last-mile deliveries and disaster management, and her research integrates optimization theory and real-world applications, addressing pressing social and environmental challenges. With a passion for bridging methodological rigor and societal impact, she aims to continue pursuing research that contributes to sustainable and resilient systems.
