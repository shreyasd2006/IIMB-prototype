## Summary
Maya Ganesh an Assistant Professor of Operations Management in the Production and Operations Management area at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore . She received her doctoral degree in O perations M anagement from the Indian School of Business, Hyderabad . Her major research interests are around public sector operations, welfare benefit programs, sustainable and socially responsible operations, food supply chains, and the impact of information technology & digitization on supply chains. Prior to joining IIMB, s he served as an Assistant Professor at the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad .
