## Summary
Professor Sachin's researcher of Information Systems (IS) and hold a PhD in IS from the Terry College of Business, University of Georgia. My primary interest is in how firms can use IS for effective knowledge creation and sharing, better decision making, and innovative problem-solving. Methodologically, I am comfortable with both quantitative and qualitative approaches. My work has been published in premier IS outlets such as Journal of the Association for Information Systems (JAIS, ABDC A*).
