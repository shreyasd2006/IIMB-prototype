## Summary
Anirudh Dhawan is an Assistant Professor of Finance at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore. He holds a PhD in Finance from the University of Technology Sydney. His primary research interests include behavioral finance, financial markets, and cryptocurrencies. His research has been published in international academic journals including the Review of Finance , Journal of Business Finance & Accounting , Abacus , and has been covered by financial news outlets such as the Australian Financial Review and Yahoo Finance. He serves as a Subject Editor for the Journal of International Financial Markets, Institutions and Money .
