The Post Graduate Programme in Management leading to:

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

The Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) is a master’s level programme, leading to the grant of a degree of Master of Business Administration (MBA). The two-year full-time residential programme is designed to equip students to take on leadership roles in an increasingly complex and dynamic global scenario. It lasts six trimesters, spread over two years, with a summer project intermediary.

IIMB benchmarks its curriculum against the best in the world to ensure that it delivers quality business education, based on the best pedagogic practices. The course content and design are inspired by management practices backed by a strong fundamental framework with an orientation towards contemporary and emerging management issues. The programme lays the foundation for conceptual and analytical reasoning and gives the students an insight into the dynamics of business. It revolves around the principle that business leaders are not mass-produced; they are nurtured and developed with care. IIMB believes in building leaders through holistic, transformative and innovative education.

IIMB has multi-disciplinary Centres of Excellence that focus on different areas such as Capital Markets, Corporate Governance & Citizenship, Entrepreneurship, Public Policy, Supply Chain Management, etc.

At the core of IIMB is its faculty, a 140-plus group of highly qualified researchers, academicians and practitioners from universities and industries worldwide. The faculty members generate knowledge through cutting-edge research and consulting in all functional areas of management.

The IIMB Edge

IIMB's curricula, innovative teaching methods, exposure to industry-best practices and experiential learning emphasize the distinctive challenges of managing in the global context. Various clubs, social projects, action-oriented courses, contemporary studies and student self-governance initiatives help develop critical thinking and leadership skills.

The PGP has nurtured some of the brightest young minds of India for more than 40 years. Alumni have distinguished themselves across the world in leading organizations in the domains of consulting, finance and investment banking, sales and marketing, etc. Many of our alumni have also been running successful entrepreneurial ventures. IIMB has posted a strong and sustained placements record. Multinational corporations recognize IIMB as a source of talent for their global operations.

Message from the Chairperson

Professor Manaswini Bhalla

Chairperson, Post Graduate Programme in Management

Welcome to the Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) and the Post Graduate Programme in Business Analytics (PGP-BA).

Indian Institute of Management Bangalore is counted as one of the best business schools in India and abroad. We endeavour to prepare future business leaders of the world. 

The Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) is one of our flagship programmes. It has a rigorous curriculum which is taught by world-class faculty. The programme provides you with global exposure and helps you develop an entrepreneurial mindset. The programme has two main phases: the first year of intensive course work across all major domains of management, followed by a summer internship, and the second year in which you can choose from a variety of electives and opt for an exchange programme in select international business schools.

The Post Graduate Programme in Business Analytics (PGPBA) is a programme built to develop future leaders with an analytical mindset and an understanding of the latest methodologies in analytics, allowing them to apply their learning across various functional areas of management.

Through your time at IIM Bangalore, you will get opportunities to collaborate with and learn from your peers, and engage with the illustrious alumni of the Institute. At IIM Bangalore while we pursue the highest levels of academic rigour, integrity and ethical behaviour, we give equal importance to the virtues of empathy and collaboration. Your time here is bound to be a wholesome and life-changing experience.

Disability Policy Statement

The Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB) endeavours to remove all barriers that prevent students with disabilities from realizing their potential. The objective of this policy is to provide “equal opportunity” for all students. IIMB commits to a time-bound programme to remove all barriers to access faced by students with disabilities. Access includes, but is not limited to:

  • Physical access to all parts of the campus used by students;
  • Access to teaching and reference material using appropriate technology wherever necessary;
  • Access to e-resources through the adoption of WCAG and other relevant standards.

IIMB will assess the needs of each enrolled student with disability individually and determine what needs to be done to provide the best possible educational experience for that student. An Office of Disability Services will arrange such an assessment and be the nodal point to ensure that the needs of the student are communicated to every relevant office/department/person of IIMB and fulfilled appropriately.

IIMB will take proactive steps to identify career opportunities for students with disabilities, and seek to influence recruiting organisations to adopt equal opportunity employment policies and practices.

IIMB will strive to prevent discrimination against students with disabilities. IIMB will sensitise all relevant stakeholders to the needs and challenges of students with disabilities through periodic workshops and campaigns. Any discrimination against students with disabilities or rude/insensitive behaviour will be taken seriously, and could be cause for disciplinary action against the concerned faculty member, staff member, or student. While IIMB will follow the Persons with Disabilities Act and other legal provisions, the intent of this policy is to go beyond the legal requirements to provide a truly supportive environment to students with disabilities. As a first step towards this objective, IIMB has setup a “Committee on Disability” at the institute level that will provide leadership and focus. In the future, IIMB will form partnerships with industry bodies and reputed non- governmental organisations (NGOs) working in the field

Adopted by IIMB Bangalore Faculty on July 15, 2009
