The Post Graduate Programme in Business Analytics, leading to:

Master of Business Administration (Business Analytics)

Post Graduate Programme in Business Analytics (PGPBA) is a masters level programme, leading to the grant of a degree of Master of Business Administration in Business Analytics. The two-year, full-time, residential programme is designed to equip students to take on leadership roles in using analytics and artificial intelligence to solve increasingly complex problems. It lasts six trimesters, spread over two years, with a summer project intermediary.

Message from the Chairperson

Professor Manaswini Bhalla

Chairperson – Post Graduate Programme in Business Analytics

Welcome to the Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP) and the Post Graduate Programme in Business Analytics (PGP-BA).

The Post Graduate Programme in Business Analytics (PGPBA) is a programme built to develop future leaders with an analytical mindset and an understanding of the latest methodologies in analytics, allowing them to apply their learning across various functional areas of management. The curriculum of the program is a rigorous and taught by world-class faculty. Like the PGP programme, this program also allows students to develop holistically and ensures that the students get global exposure. The first year of the program has intensive course work with emphasis on analytical and mathematical skills followed by eight week summer internship. In the second year of the programme, students can choose from a variety of electives and opt for an exchange programme in select international business schools. Through the second year, PGPBA students are encouraged to take electives in the field of business analytics across the domain of management.

Through your time at IIM Bangalore, you will get opportunities to collaborate with and learn from your peers, and engage with the illustrious alumni of the Institute. At IIM Bangalore while we pursue the highest levels of academic rigour, integrity and ethical behaviour, we give equal importance to the virtues of empathy and collaboration. Your time here is bound to be a wholesome and life-changing experience.

Disability Policy Statement

The Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB) endeavours to remove all barriers that prevent students with disabilities from realizing their potential. The objective of this policy is to provide “equal opportunity” for all students. IIMB commits to a time-bound programme to remove all barriers to access faced by students with disabilities. Access includes, but is not limited to:

  • Physical access to all parts of the campus used by students;
  • Access to teaching and reference material using appropriate technology wherever necessary;
  • Access to e-resources through the adoption of WCAG and other relevant standards.

IIMB will assess the needs of each enrolled student with disability individually and determine what needs to be done to provide the best possible educational experience for that student. An Office of Disability Services will arrange such an assessment and be the nodal point to ensure that the needs of the student are communicated to every relevant office/department/person of IIMB and fulfilled appropriately.

IIMB will take proactive steps to identify career opportunities for students with disabilities, and seek to influence recruiting organisations to adopt equal opportunity employment policies and practices.

IIMB will strive to prevent discrimination against students with disabilities. IIMB will sensitise all relevant stakeholders to the needs and challenges of students with disabilities through periodic workshops and campaigns. Any discrimination against students with disabilities or rude/insensitive behaviour will be taken seriously, and could be cause for disciplinary action against the concerned faculty member, staff member, or student. While IIMB will follow the Persons with Disabilities Act and other legal provisions, the intent of this policy is to go beyond the legal requirements to provide a truly supportive environment to students with disabilities. As a first step towards this objective, IIMB has setup a “Committee on Disability” at the institute level that will provide leadership and focus. In the future, IIMB will form partnerships with industry bodies and reputed non- governmental organisations (NGOs) working in the field

Adopted by IIMB Bangalore Faculty on July 15, 2009
