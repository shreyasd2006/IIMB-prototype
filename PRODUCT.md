# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS. Data lives in JSON files (`data/`) shaped like the future content model. There is no build step and no CMS. Pages are served from any static server. (User confirmed, 11 Oct 2026.)

## Users

- **Primary: prospective PGP and MBA applicants** (user confirmed as the tie-breaker audience). They are usually on a phone and comparing schools. Their job is to find the fee, the deadline for their batch, eligibility and the CAT route, and to choose between PGP, PGPBA, EPGP and PGPEM.
- Secondary audiences named in the brief: PhD applicants and collaborators (finding faculty by topic), journalists and policymakers (expert finder, media contact), executives and L&D buyers (Exec Ed catalogue), recruiters and partners, alumni, and visitors who need statutory information (RTI, annual reports).

## Product Purpose

A working concept prototype of a redesigned www.iimb.ac.in. It tests two ideas from the audit (`../IIMB Website Redesign Audit and Strategic Direction.md`, section 7):
1. A fact-led programme page and finder fix the applicant journey.
2. The "Open Corridor" system can look distinctive while carrying very dense information.

The prototype succeeds if the six timed tasks in section 7.7 can each be completed in one or two steps.

## Positioning

IIMB is where rigorous management thinking meets the questions India is actually working on, and does so in the open. The proof belongs to IIMB alone: the FT teaching-case ranking (11th globally), the 747-paper working-paper series, the CDPG and CPP centres, NSRCEL, and B.V. Doshi's 1983 stone campus.

## Operating Context

- Applicants arrive during CAT season, often on mobile data.
- Journalists need an expert quickly.
- Statutory content (RTI, tenders, the IIM Act) must stay complete and reachable from every page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Facts render from one data file, the stand-in for the facts register. Every number carries its source URL and date.
- One newsroom data set. Every listing is a filtered view of it.
- Events move to "past" automatically, based on the visitor's date.
- Banned: carousels for primary content, autoplay video, animated counters, "Read more", stock photography, decorative gradients, and icon-only links without accessible names.
- Unknown or contradicted facts appear as visible placeholders, for example "[Fee for 2027-29: confirm with Admissions]".
- Every page is marked "Prototype, not the official IIMB website".

## Brand Commitments

- IIMB's sun symbol, designed by NID Ahmedabad and in use since 1994. The official logo lockup is reproduced from iimb.ac.in (PNG; no vector master is published), reversed for dark bands. The site's single accent red (#B12322) is sampled from it (user-approved, 11 Oct 2026).
- Campus identity: B.V. Doshi's campus, completed in 1983, modelled on Fatehpur Sikri, with stone-lined colonnades.

## Evidence on Hand

- The audit report (Markdown and PDF), with cited IIMB URLs as of 10 Oct 2026.
- **Not available:** commissioned campus photography (Creative Commons campus photos are used instead), a vector logo master and brand guidelines, campus drawings, licensed fonts, and faculty consent for real profiles. The prototype must not fabricate quotes, testimonials, people, rankings or statistics.

## Product Principles

1. Show a person, a question or a place before a claim.
2. Facts are first-class: owned, dated and sourced.
3. Every page has a facts zone and a story zone, in a fixed order.
4. Young means present-tense and specific, not decorative.
5. One institution with many voices: programmes and centres get sub-identities, not separate sites.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum (GIGW 3.0 mapping to be confirmed): keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion, one H1 per page, 4.5:1 text contrast, meaningful alt text, and Devanagari designed in. Designed at 360 px first.
