## Scope and mode

The whole site is in scope. Home, About, Campus Life and Industry run in **Persuade/Experience** mode. Faculty, Research, News and Centres run in **Operate/Read** mode, for directories and stories. Home is the primary surface. The direction is pinned by the user's brief ("The Architectural Institution", built on the base HTML file). No concept-seed was rolled, and the seed key is "pinned-by-brief". The build is code-led because image generation is unavailable.

## Direction contract

THESIS: IIMB as stone and light. Doshi's campus is a lesson in "rhythm and composition", and the sun symbol is its light. The site refuses the category default: a stock hero with a centred slogan above three equal cards, logo strips and a ranking ticker.

OWN-WORLD:
- Ground: soft white #F6F5F1, with cut granite bands (#DAD6CC) and a black (#191919) "night stone" band.
- IIMB red #B12322 is the light. It marks the active state, primary actions, focus, and one drenched red band on Industry.
- Olive #777B50 carries courtyard greenery, as tags. Bronze #B18A58 carries sunlit stone, as hairlines and numerals.
- Type: Martel (Indian serif, weights 200–300) for display, and the variable-width Anek Latin for UI and labels.
- Detailing: 1px rules, square corners, no shadows, and pergola-bar patterns.

STORY: The visitor sees the campus moving, understands that IIMB is a place and a body of ideas, and then picks a door: programmes, research, faculty, centres, news or industry.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- The official campus film runs full-bleed inside an inset stone frame.
- A Martel 200 headline, "Let our study be enlightening.", sits lower-left, with the Devanagari motto above it in small type.
- A pause control sits lower-right.
- A slim index of six doors runs along the bottom edge.
- The primary action is "Explore programmes".

FORM: Architectural editorial built on fine rules, asymmetric grids and courtyards of negative space. The signature interaction is the **pergola reveal**: images arrive through bars of light, a masked stripe sweep. The video frame also opens from an inset to full-bleed as you scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

The film plays behind the motto, and the pergola-bar reveal carries through every page.
