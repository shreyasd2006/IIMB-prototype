About this concept

This is a concept website for the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore, built under the brief “The Architectural Institution”. It is not the official site and is not endorsed by IIMB.

All text about the institute, its people, programmes, research, news and events was gathered from iimb.ac.in and its official subdomains on 16 October 2026. Quotations are verbatim. News and event text reproduces IIMB’s releases. Faculty data comes from the eleven area pages and the official profiles. Where official pages contradict each other, both values are noted rather than one chosen.

The IIMB logo and sun symbol are reproduced unaltered from iimb.ac.in. The campus films are IIMB’s own: two video files from the IIMB homepage, and YouTube films from the official channel and Campus Tour page. Faculty portraits are deliberately not used.

Before any public release: confirm rights for official photographs and film, obtain higher-resolution archive images and a vector logo master, and re-verify dated facts such as rankings, fees and the board list.
