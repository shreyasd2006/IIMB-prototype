The Indian Institute of Management Bangalore is a leading graduate school of management in Asia. Under the IIM Act of 2017, IIMB is an Institute of National Importance. Its programmes run from undergraduate degrees to a PhD, alongside executive education for working leaders.

The campus was laid out by B.V. Doshi on the plan of Fatehpur Sikri: a network of corridors, courtyards and gardens, so that academic exchange carries on beyond the classroom.
