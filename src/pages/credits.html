Sources and credits

This is a design concept for IIM Bangalore, not the official website. Its facts, people, papers, stories and films come from IIMB’s own pages and channels.

Content reused from the sibling concept was fetched from iimb.ac.in on 16 October 2026; CSITM pages and faculty portraits were captured on 18 October 2026. Facts change: always check the official site before acting on a date or fee.

What is editorial

  • The concept name “The Global Leadership Journal”, its section titles, and short headlines such as “Founded 1973. Still being written.”
  • Audience groupings in the programme finder (based on each programme’s published eligibility).
  • Research themes and their keyword matching, the theme–centre map, and the centre colours. These are labelled “derived” where shown.
  • Colours follow the IIMB Color Guide supplied for this redesign: red (#A92D2D), maroon (#631111) and cream (#E8E3DB), with white, black and greys.
  • The student story slot on Campus Life is an empty, labelled placeholder: no testimonial is invented.

Brand

The sun-symbol logo (NID Ahmedabad, in use since 1994) is the official IIMB logo file supplied for this redesign, used unaltered: the colour version on white and cream, the white version on maroon.

Faculty portraits

The 109 portraits are the ones IIMB publishes on its area-wise faculty pages and individual profiles, centre-cropped and resized. If one fails to load, initials are shown.

Films

The campus and library films are the official films on the iimb.ac.in homepage; the stills used as posters are frames from them. YouTube films are from the official IIM Bangalore channel and load only on click (titles verified via oEmbed, 17 October 2026).
